VALUE TYPE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

value_type Field (0 or 1):
  • 0 = Fixed Amount (e.g., $10 off)
  • 1 = Percentage (e.g., 20% off)

Calculation Logic:
  Fixed Amount: discount = promo/discount->value
  Percentage: discount = (orderAmount * value) / 100
  
Discount Cap (for Discounts only):
  For percentage discounts, max_discount_amount applies
  Example: 20% off with $25 max = never exceeds $25
